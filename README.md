### 💼 Work at AnoCloud

#### 🧑‍💼 Karmik: HRMS for Indian companies
`NestJS` `Next.js` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL` `GCP`
- Designed a **53-screen prototype** for 4 roles (Founder, HR, Manager, Employee) and the system architecture
- **Two-step payroll** (HR prepares, Founder approves) processes **500 employees in under 30 seconds**; payslips sent automatically
- **Self-service onboarding links** replace paper forms; exits are checklist-driven with automatic relieving letters
- Launched with **610 backend + 188 browser tests** passing; 2FA, role-based access, masked ID and bank data

#### 📨 Saarthi: AI-assisted sales outreach platform
`NestJS` `Next.js` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL` `Groq`
- **Warm-up gate** (visit, like, comment) unlocks a connection request only after real engagement
- **AI message drafts** in 3 angles with brand-voice checks, and **a human approves every message**
- Follow-up reminders at 3, 7 and 15 days; 2FA, RBAC, rate limits and an audit trail; **700+ tests**

#### 🛒 Retail Home Delivery Platform (retail chain client)
`NestJS` `Next.js` `Flutter` `PostgreSQL` `AWS`
- Built the **POS sync service** (checkpoint/resume, selective repair), syncing store sales every 20 min; holds **3.6M+ receipts**
- Daily **SAP stock snapshots** and KPI queries (sales, stock-outs, supplier fill rates) for business dashboards

#### 🏥 More client work
- **[Hospital Visitor Tracking System](https://github.com/Anup1603/HVM_Backend)**: QR gate passes with visitor photos for multi-branch hospital chains
- **Multi-tenant Invoice Platform**: GST invoicing with separate data per company, used by **10+ companies**
